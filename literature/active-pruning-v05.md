# Active-list pruning recommendations, v0.5

Reviewed 2026-10-30. Recommend **14 entries leave the active task list and remain in the evidence archive**, with **100 retained candidates**, including every measured/newly admitted task. The [per-ID JSON](active-pruning-v05.json) covers all 114 registry entries, cites their primary sources, records review depth and gives reactivation conditions. This recommendation does not modify the catalog, results or website.

`KEEP_CANDIDATE` is not a claim that acquisition, licensing, scoring or execution is complete. `REMOVE_ACTIVE` means the current row is unsuitable as a concrete active evaluation unit; it does not mean its data are bad, its topic irrelevant, or its experiment universally impossible. No recommendation uses measured performance to select tasks. B01, G01, H08 and J08 are explicitly protected alongside all existing measurements.

This is selective pruning. Concrete high-effort tasks such as voltage extraction, HippoUnit validation, 3D pose, MRI reconstruction, tractography and connectomics remain candidates when upstream endpoints and protocols are identifiable. Their unattempted runtime/data acquisition is not evidence of infeasibility. NeuroGym M03 and MyoChallenge M06 also remain candidates because upstream task/reward settings are concrete; executing them still requires an appropriate controller/rollout protocol. Generic ecological image/video tasks are not removed solely for being adjacent: laboratory behavior measurement can depend on cross-species transfer, and their domain shift is already documented.

Archiving placeholders reduces count inflation; it does **not** solve coverage gaps. Adaptive experimental design, mechanistic posterior recovery, general brain alignment, some clinical EEG targets and broad embodied simulation remain incompletely instantiated. Those gaps must remain visible centrally and in the archive. Kept suites/workflow candidates still need a precise run definition before entering any cross-task performance denominator.

Review depth is bounded: previous source-linked v0.4 admission audits and registry evidence were reused; primary NeuralBench, OmniEEG-Bench, EEG-Arena and Brain-Score pages were reread for the umbrella decisions. This is not an exhaustive literature, data-rights or runtime audit. Before integration, protect any additional tasks that became measured while this review was running.
