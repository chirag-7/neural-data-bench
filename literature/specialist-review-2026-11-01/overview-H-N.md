# Current specialist review: measured families H–N

Reviewed on 1 November 2026, with that date as the literature cutoff. This review covers all 16 measured H–N tasks: H01, H02, H08, H16, I07, I10, J08, K01, K04, K05, K06, L04, L06, M01, M04 and N05. The corresponding JSON files record primary sources, dates, search queries, information compatibility, existing-reference limitations and the next defensible comparison. No model was run, checkpoint downloaded, frozen contract changed or score imported.

**None of these custom slices has a verified current global winner.** Fifteen have relevant strong candidates without a matched winner; N05 is a deterministic correctness fixture for which a best predictive model is not an appropriate concept. This is an evidence conclusion, not a claim that the existing references are equally strong. H16 and L04 have particularly clear gaps between their simple conventional references and feasible specialist comparisons. H02, K04, J08 and L06 already contain substantive domain references, although each has specific limitations.

`VERIFIED_ON_PROTOCOL` means an actual implementation or archived prediction has been audited on the exact local inputs, split and scorer. It does not mean exhaustive current optimality. Candidate entries keep `matched_to_our_slice=false` until such an execution exists; `information_match` separately explains whether a candidate could be adapted without gaining extra inputs. The main `current_best_claim` is therefore `STRONG_CANDIDATES_NO_MATCHED_WINNER` for the 15 predictive/inference tasks, and `UNRESOLVED` for N05. Existing local comparators can have Tier A evidence even though current optimality is unresolved.

## What the current literature changes

The EEG comparison should include contemporary models rather than treating a conventional decoder as the field ceiling. [CBraMod](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/bbbd6d915cb90be21c1254a82d45cedd-Abstract-Conference.html), [REVE](https://brain-bzh.github.io/reve/) and [MIRepNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.20254) are substantive candidates. However, the updated [EEG-FM-Compass comparison](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.17883) finds conditional rankings and competitive specialists. Neither a foundation-model label nor a broad benchmark lead establishes an H01/H02 win. The [official MOABB BI2012 table](https://moabb.neurotechx.com/docs/paper_results.html) evaluates within-session folds, not this project's held-out-person AUC.

Sleep is moving quickly. [SleepGPT](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-67970-4) supplies relevant channel-adaptive evidence, while the released [SleepFM-Clinical repository](https://github.com/zou-group/sleepfm-clinical) supplies a staging checkpoint. [SleepFM-2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06849) is a current preprint, not a confirmed executable single-Fpz-Cz comparator in this review. Additional channels, demographic inputs, label-based night trimming and SleepEDF pretraining exposure must remain visible. Likewise, recent single-channel ocular denoisers make H16's Wiener baseline an especially incomplete specialist comparison; their published scores still cannot be transferred across mixture, source-component and normalization policies.

The cell/molecular category contains several distinct tasks. K01 is scalar electrophysiology-to-morphology classification, K04 is supervised layer transfer without spatial coordinates, K05 is supervised calibration to adjacent-section measurements, and K06 is genetically labeled extracellular waveform classification. [TabICLv2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11139) and [TabPFN-3.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17895) are current numerical contenders for appropriate slices, but not domain-specific winners. [Nicheformer](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02814-z) and other molecular foundations need compatible gene identities and preprocessing. A [2026 harmonized benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17227) reinforces that task and modality change the ordering. Spatial clustering ARI, multimodal layer prediction and anonymous expression-only F1 are different comparisons.

For K05, the original [orthogonally validated source study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11978107/) supports Bisque and hspe among its evaluated reference-based methods. The recent [DECONVersation preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.02.756356v1.full) adds foundation-assisted contenders, but these require a cell reference and gene identities absent from the executed contract. The existing three IF/RNAScope fractions remain the target; replacing them with snRNA-derived proportions would remove the independent target that makes this slice useful.

For K06, [HIPPIE's official model card](https://huggingface.co/Jesusgf23/hippie) identifies both an information mismatch and possible source exposure: the released multimodal checkpoint uses waveform/ISI/ACG and its pretraining corpus includes CellExplorer and Allen Visual Coding. A waveform-only ablation and exact-unit overlap audit are necessary before clean matched claims. [PhysMAP](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-71331-0) is also relevant current work, but its full multimodal performance is not available from K06's waveform-only inputs. CEED remains a substantive older contender; its 2025 PMC availability must not be confused with its 2023 publication.

J08 has a more direct specialist lineage than a generic microscopy leaderboard suggests. The [2026 SynAPSeg source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13432752/) compares punctum-trained StarDist and Cellpose-SAM. Current [Cellpose-SAM v2 and CellposeDINO](https://cellpose.readthedocs.io/en/latest/models.html) warrant investigation, but cell-body generalization does not establish tiny-punctum superiority. SAM prompts derived from hidden expert masks would give extra information. The next useful comparison is clean training on the allowed fields and annotation coverage, not a paper-number substitution.

L06 and M04 have tractable specified likelihoods. The current [sbi toolchain](https://sbi.readthedocs.io/en/stable/how_to_guide/06_choosing_inference_method.html), [OneFlowSBI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22951), [GenSBI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27499) and [BayesFlow 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07098) are important current resources, but amortized training and simulation budgets would create a different comparison. A known-likelihood sampler is a stronger immediate L06 control. [HSSM's current releases](https://github.com/lnccbrown/HSSM/releases) genuinely support choice-only reinforcement learning, yet an implementation must match M04's exact softmax rule rather than substitute an RLDDM. Its primary MAE also motivates an openly declared-prior posterior-median comparator; this is a mathematical proposal, not an established published winner.

## Recommended order and evidence limits

Prioritize prospective comparator additions for H16, L04, J08 and H08, where task-specific alternatives are clear and existing coverage is most incomplete. Add current tabular or waveform comparisons for K01, I10 and K06, with source exposure checks before pretrained K06 use. Keep SingleR in K04 and PG in L06 as substantive references. Keep K05's supervised scope explicit and create a separate reference-deconvolution contract if that scientific question is desired. Improve M01 within its available histories; align M04's statistical reference with its loss. I07's missing raw physiology and session confound warrant a source/protocol redesign before a foundation-model comparison. N05 needs additional fixtures, not a predictive-model upgrade.

These are recommendations about the next defensible comparison, not post hoc replacements of measured references. Historical scores and failures must remain intact. Any new fitted method needs pinned software/weights, a declared external-data and compute budget, training-only selection, unchanged held-out labels and scorer, and a distinct amended result. Equal runtime alone does not equalize pretraining, method development or historical test exposure.

The search was bounded but covered recent primary papers, official repositories/model cards and relevant benchmark documentation for every listed task. It did not infer adoption from stars, marketing claims or secondary leaderboards. Direct access was incomplete for some sources: MDPI returned 429 for selected denoising/Means Field pages; bioRxiv returned 403 for DECONVersation despite an indexed primary extract; Nature authentication redirects and a PMC CAPTCHA affected PhysMAP/HIPPIE; EEG-Arena's dynamic ranking table did not yield a stable extract. These limits and unverified runnable artifacts are recorded in the task files. Publication dates are distinct from access dates; unknown dates remain null or explicitly year-level.

Exact-name searches for “DynaMEchs” and related cognitive/SBI phrases identified the [legacy multibody dynamics library](https://dynamechs.sourceforge.net/), not a verified modern neuroscience benchmark. Its intended identity remains unresolved. No unrelated mechanics result has been relabeled as cognitive or mechanistic-neuroscience evidence.
