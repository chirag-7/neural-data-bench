# Bounded behavior, anatomy and neural-population review, v0.4

Checked 2026-10-30. This extension assesses **56 task–resource pairings across seven families**, not 56 independent datasets or completed experiments. [Admission records](admission-v04-behavior-population.json) give each ID its sources, concrete reason and review depth: 4 ready, 40 source-only and 12 deferred. [Search records](searches-v04-behavior-population.jsonl) preserve 11 exact queries and 26 primary-resource inspections or acquisition attempts. These logs were reconstructed from this extension’s tool activity; they are not a prospectively registered or exhaustive systematic review. Most source-only entries were not downloaded, so absence of an executable adapter is not evidence of poor data quality or universal infeasibility.

## What became executable

[A09](../experiments/A09/protocol.md) uses human CalMS21 social-behavior labels with measured/estimated pose inputs and the official animal-disjoint recording split. It is a fixed 3 Hz numerical slice, not raw-video perception. [A14](../experiments/A14/protocol.md) uses real Rat7M motion-capture coordinates with controlled artificial gaps; it tests measured-trajectory recovery rather than video pose estimation. Both retain sequence/animal grouping and data checksums. CalMS21’s current record and original paper disagree on license wording; the discrepancy is preserved and raw data remain in ignored cache. [CalMS21 data](https://doi.org/10.22002/D1.1991), [DISK data release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15800034).

[E01](../experiments/E01/protocol.md) follows official NLB tensor conventions for MC_Maze_Small, using pinned public NWB files and the test targets released in January 2026. The supervised diagnostic combines train/validation trials and explicitly logs the final training trial omitted by the official reader. Its scorer matches official bits/spike, and an independent count extraction verifies input ordering/binning. It supports same-session held-out-trial inference, not cross-animal transfer or a full NLB leaderboard reproduction. C05 was already measured and was not rerun by this admission audit. [Published DANDI release](https://doi.org/10.48324/dandi.000140/0.220113.0408), [official NLB tools and release note](https://github.com/neurallatents/nlb_tools).

## Closest prior work and distinctions

**SciVid (ICCV 2025)** already tests general pretrained video representations against domain baselines across scientific tasks, including CalMS21 and FlyVsFly. Its trainable readouts make it close prior work for the proposed general-versus-specialist question. It tests video foundation representations, while the current Sol experiments test code synthesis followed by numerical fitting. These should remain separate evaluation tracks. Its official repository also states that CalMS21 was filtered/downsampled with an additional validation holdout; its published scores cannot be inserted into our fixed A09 center/metric table. [Primary paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Hasson_SciVid_Cross-Domain_Evaluation_of_Video_Models_in_Scientific_Applications_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf), [official repository](https://github.com/google-deepmind/scivid).

**HiDRA / Days of our Mice (2026-10-18 preprint)** highlights cross-lab variation in behavioral definitions and provides lab-specific classifier heads. It is relevant to annotation transfer, reporting agreement between labs and choosing deployment definitions. Its CalMS21 distinction requires care: trajectories were used in self-supervised representation learning, while behavior labels were excluded from supervised classifier training; threshold-calibrated and uncalibrated results also differ. Thus label-held-out transfer is not unseen-domain pretraining, and threshold calibration is an adaptation step. We located primary text/project evidence but did not reproduce its scores or establish it as current SOTA. [Primary preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.18.758876v1.full), [primary project](https://neuroethology.github.io/hidra-site/).

An [archived VASSI reference](../experiments/A09/archived-vassi/README.md) is documented separately. It supplies predictions on identical A09 centers, but uses larger training/context/optimization budgets. Its archive comparison must remain distinct from strict equal-budget win counts.

## Remaining gaps and admission policy

No anatomy image endpoint was prepared in this bounded audit. BigNeuron SWC-only statistics would not evaluate optical-image tracing; estimated registration transforms would not replace expert alignment ground truth. Volumetric segmentation, multiview pose, speech BCI and dynamic encoding need additional data and modality-specific infrastructure. Source-only candidates remain available for that work.

The audit also identified annotation gates: algorithm-generated calcium traces, cell matches or motion estimates cannot silently become truth for competitors. Synthetic or hybrid constructions remain valid only when explicitly labeled and when original measured inputs, mask/insertion generation and evaluation truth are separated. Failed access is recorded only where attempted; budget deferrals are release decisions, not claims that a benchmark is impossible.

This review improves traceability and spans behavior, anatomy, extracellular signals, calcium, population activity, sensory encoding and BCI. It does **not** demonstrate that every neuroscience laboratory need or every published benchmark has been covered. Expert domain review and a prospective search/update protocol remain necessary before a completeness claim in a paper.
