# Calcium batch v11 admission evidence

C06 is acquired and prepared: every CASCADE DS04 OGB1 zebrafish pDp neuron (15 cells, 45 recordings, 81.66 minutes), 15,077,557 original bytes. The fixed custom split holds out two acute specimen groups (8 neurons, 24 recordings) from two training groups (7 neurons, 21 recordings). Targets come from simultaneous electrical event times, not a calcium-model output. See `experiments/C06/protocol.md`, `data-manifest.json` and `validation.json`. Native-rate input and equal-animal correlation differ explicitly from published noise-resampled CASCADE results. Source root GPL-3.0 is preserved; no separate data-specific license was located, so raw data remains a local ignored cache. The uncontaminated fixed reference available here is conventional OASIS, not CASCADE SOTA.

C01 is deferred. The [original README](https://github.com/codeneuro/neurofinder) exposes annotated training recordings and unannotated official test movies. The [dataset-license issue #28](https://github.com/codeneuro/neurofinder/issues/28) remains open with zero replies (API verified). The [publisher dataset guide](https://github.com/codeneuro/neurofinder-datasets) describes around 1GB zipped per recording. A custom heldout-training-recording endpoint remains feasible after resolving dataset terms and implementing the [official center matcher](https://github.com/codeneuro/neurofinder-python); a generic image-IoU scorer would change the scientific question. No large movie downloads were incurred without a ready contract. This is a bounded admission deferral, not proof that every reuse is forbidden.

C09 was checked as a fallback. The [CNMF-E paper](https://elifesciences.org/articles/28728) and its [CC0 Dryad record](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.kr17k) provide four real movies, totaling 1.88GB. Exact ground-truth recovery experiments in the paper are simulated. The [reproduction repository](https://github.com/zhoupc/eLife_submission), pinned at f23d80e8f56033040ae06e0810d05f053bd78807, contains only .gitignore under data/ and results/. No independently annotated real-movie target set was located in these primary releases. Treating CNMF-E components as true neurons would create pseudo-ground-truth. Reactivate with independent ROI/spike labels, or explicitly authorize a separate simulated endpoint.

No methods were scored, no models called, and no central registry/site/frozen artifact/commit was changed. Full source URLs, exact blocker and reactivation conditions are in `batch-v11-calcium-evidence.json`.
