**B03 — SpikeInterface decentralized registration.** Fully synthetic SpikeInterface drifting-voltage simulations: 4 train/4 held-out seeds, 90s each. Processed detected/localized peak-count rasters and rigid imposed displacement; not in-vivo motion or full DREDge reproduction. Official decentralized-registration routines are used on this custom front end. No held-out sign/scale/time alignment; only additive translation gauge removed. [Protocol](../experiments/B03/protocol.md), [results](../results/B03.json), [independent audit](../results/audits/B03-batch-v11.json).

At most 50 us/event timing error, exact labels/order; intentionally timing-lossy. Spikepack 0.1.0 is original-code local execution on another source, not its original IBL benchmark. Two heapq whitelist failures are retained; exact exclusion was ambiguous in broad prompt utility wording. [Protocol](../experiments/B08/protocol.md), [results](../results/B08.json), [independent audit](../results/verification/B08-independent-final.json).

**C06 — Default AR1 OASIS + training global count scale.** All 15 CASCADE DS04 OGB1 zebrafish neurons/45 recordings; 7 neurons/21 train recordings from 2 fish, 8 neurons/24 test recordings from 2 other fish. Custom native-rate 200 ms-smoothed count/Pearson endpoint, not paper benchmark. Default AR1 OASIS with training count scaling; public CASCADE global weights include held-out source and are not used. Source repository GPL3; no separate data-specific license declaration located, so raw arrays stay local. [Protocol](../experiments/C06/protocol.md), [results](../results/C06.json), [independent audit](../results/audits/C06-batch-v11.json).

## Reviews, audits and interpretation

All 21 generated submissions received [read-only code/AST review](../literature/batch-v11-code-review.json), with scores blinded and no code execution by the reviewer. This is observational review, not proof. Independent saved-output audits reconstruct original source/target mappings and metrics without rerunning fitting. B08 decodes retained blobs in fresh namespaces; two rejected programs have no fabricated score. A15 audit passes numeric fidelity while preserving the 268 line compliance failure. Earlier source-acquisition transport/audit invocation issues remain logged; audit-only path resolution fixes do not alter frozen methods or scores.

C06 illustrates why strong correlation is insufficient: one held-out neuron's relative signed count bias reaches 7.489847/24.107274/16.543687 for the three general models and 201.679029 for OASIS. Bias is(sum prediction−sum truth)/sum truth; 1 means 100% overcount. These are secondary source-neuron diagnostics, not changed primary endpoints. High Pearson must not be described as calibrated spike-count recovery.

Point ordering is mixed: GPT-5.6 andGPT-6 beat the B03 reference whileGPT-6.1 does not; GPT-6.1 alone beats K05 ridge; all three beat the K06 reference. GPT-6.1 leads E06 main, butGPT-6 leads taxis. Only the two compliant A15 programs support strict comparisons. B08's successful generated codec is larger than Spikepack. No current global best, matched-total-effort superiority, statistical significance, stable model-version trend or exhaustive coverage is claimed. Native metrics are never averaged across tasks.
