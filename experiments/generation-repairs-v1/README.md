## Interpretation

These points measure manually repaired procedures. Optimizer changes preserve the stated training objective but may change numerical convergence and predictions; they are not byte-identical executions. J08 GPT-4.1 additionally caps per-tree bootstrap draws at 250000 rather than 16451616 to meet the unchanged 180-second computation limit. This manual training-budget adaptation changes fitting and cannot be attributed to the original model alone. Compatibility/shape fixes may enable an original method without improving its scientific quality. A valid low score stays low: no test-score-driven algorithm replacement was performed. The repaired panel cannot establish that an untouched model would debug itself or outperform specialists autonomously.
